| Metric                | Meaning                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **`segm_mAP`**        | Mean Average Precision across IoU thresholds from **0.50 to 0.95 (step=0.05)**. This is the **main COCO score** — a summary of model quality. |
| **`segm_mAP_50`**     | AP at a fixed **IoU = 0.50** — often considered a **"loose" match**, more lenient.                                                            |
| **`segm_mAP_75`**     | AP at a fixed **IoU = 0.75** — a **stricter** metric, penalizes rough masks.                                                                  |
| **`segm_mAP_small`**  | AP for **small** objects (area < 32×32 pixels). Tests how well your model detects tiny features.                                              |
| **`segm_mAP_medium`** | AP for **medium** objects (32×32 to 96×96).                                                                                                   |
| **`segm_mAP_large`**  | AP for **large** objects (area > 96×96). Shows how well big masks are handled.                                                                |


Use segm_mAP as the main evaluation metric.

mAP_50 and mAP_75 give you more detailed insight.

small, medium, large show where your model performs well or poorly (e.g., maybe it misses small bubbles?).
